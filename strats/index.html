Invest and flip

From WizChamp's 3.30 wealth plan

Rules

  • Put 20–50% of income into holds.
  • Buy days 1–3. Sell at the spikes: day 3, day 7, week 2–3.
  • Average return: 2–2.5× in a week, 3–5× in two.
  • Always have something listed.

Early sellers

  • Life + resistance rares, more with chaos res or suppression.
  • Rog crafts (nerfed, still decent). Buy his logbooks.
  • Cluster jewels for the top builds.
  • Stygian Vise / Cord Belt + Essence of Envy.
